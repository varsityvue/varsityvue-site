# VarsityVue Week 6 Pick ’Em — proposed Official Rules

1. **Sponsor and contest.** The sponsor and operator is [full legal entity name, postal address, and contact email] (“VarsityVue”). The Week 6 Pick ’Em contest is a free football prediction contest. Facebook and Meta do not sponsor, endorse, administer, or associate themselves with it. Entrants release Meta from claims arising from this promotion to the extent permitted by law.

2. **No purchase.** No purchase, payment, donation, subscription, or paid account is required. Buying anything will not improve a participant’s standing. Internet access and a free VarsityVue account are required.

3. **Eligibility.** Legal residents of Texas who are at least 18 years old at entry. VarsityVue owners, employees, administrators, moderators, household members, and anyone administering or grading the contest are ineligible. [Confirm operator/household exclusions with counsel.] Void where prohibited. Each entrant must use an account and number they control. One valid entry per natural person for the week, regardless of the number of accounts, devices, or phone numbers.

4. **Contest period and entry cutoff.** The Week 6 slate opens when VarsityVue publishes it at [date/time, America/Chicago]. A new entry must be fully saved before the earliest scheduled kickoff among included games, as displayed on the contest page. The authoritative cutoff is the corresponding stored instant; “before” excludes the exact kickoff instant. The slate and first kickoff must be settled before opening entries. Existing entrants may edit selections for games that have not locked, subject to the weekly closure [currently Friday at 7:00 p.m. America/Chicago]. A game locks at its displayed authoritative kickoff. The Game of the Week prediction locks at that game’s kickoff. The weekly close may prevent further edits even if a game’s kickoff is later. [Confirm actual Week 6 schedule and closure.]

5. **How to enter.** Create or sign into a free VarsityVue account, provide a U.S. mobile phone number, select a winner for every included matchup, predict one integer from 0 to 300 for the **combined points scored by both teams** in the designated Game of the Week, and save. A success confirmation and an initial completed-entry time indicate receipt. A registration alone, saved draft, partial slate, missing prediction, unsaved change, or entry submitted at or after the cutoff does not qualify. Draft picks may be saved before the entry cutoff, but have no contest status, prize effect, or completed-entry timestamp. A phone number’s format is normalized and checked for reuse across accounts. VarsityVue does **not** currently verify that the entrant owns that number or that it is a mobile line. Entrants must keep account and contact details accurate.

6. **Edits and locks.** A qualified entrant may change an unlocked pick and, before the Game of the Week locks, its total-points prediction. An edit does not change the original completed-entry time. Locked selections cannot be changed. A failed save leaves the prior valid entry in effect. VarsityVue may correct an administrative scoring or schedule error with an audit trail; entrants do not gain an exception to a past lock.

7. **Scoring.** Each correct pick in a verified, non-void game earns one point. An incorrect pick earns zero. A void game earns neither a point nor a loss. The official winner of a verified forfeit controls that matchup even if no ordinary score is posted. A played overtime game uses its official final winner and full final score, including every overtime period. Authoritative verified score corrections can change grading and standings.

8. **Ranking and tiebreakers.** Rank valid entrants by (a) most correct picks, then (b) the smallest absolute difference between the entrant’s Game of the Week combined-points prediction and the authoritative combined final score, then (c) earliest initial valid completed-entry timestamp. If recorded timestamps are identical, the database’s immutable receipt order resolves the remaining tie. A later edit does not change this order. If the designated Game of the Week has no verified played final with both scores, skip (b) for every entrant and use (c).

9. **Schedule exceptions.** A cancelled game, no contest, or verified tie with no pickable winner is void for pick scoring. A postponed or rescheduled game remains pending while its official outcome can be determined. **Proposed owner decision:** if no verified pickable outcome is recorded by Tuesday 11:59 p.m. America/Chicago immediately following the scheduled Week 6 Friday, void that matchup for this contest; later play does not reopen the finalized contest. This is not implemented yet. If the Game of the Week is cancelled, void, forfeited without a played score, or otherwise has no verified played total by that resolution time, the first tiebreaker is skipped. VarsityVue will not replace the designated Game of the Week after entries open. Material schedule changes will be posted on the contest page. Thus 23 valid entries yield $23, while 100 or more yield $100. Registered accounts, partial submissions, disqualified entries, administrator/test entries, and duplicate or fraudulent entries do not count. The displayed amount is provisional until eligibility review and finalization. No entry fee is deducted. Payment will use a mutually agreed electronic method, with VarsityVue retaining a reasonable alternative payment option. [Counsel should review payment eligibility and any reporting obligations.] No substitution or transfer except where law requires.

VarsityVue will contact the prospective winner using the account email or supplied number within [owner-approved notification interval] after finalization. The prospective winner must respond and supply information reasonably necessary for payment within 72 hours after the first notification. If ineligible, unreachable, or unresponsive, VarsityVue may disqualify the entry and repeat the same ranking procedure. No payment is required to claim a prize. If the prospective winner does not qualify or respond in time, VarsityVue repeats the ranking and contact process with the next eligible entrant. Technical failures do not extend a game lock or guarantee receipt of an unsaved submission.
