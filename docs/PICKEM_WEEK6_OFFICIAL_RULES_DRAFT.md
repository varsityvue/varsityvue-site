2. **No purchase.** No purchase, payment, donation, subscription, or paid account with VarsityVue or Gilder Storage is required. Purchasing from, contacting, renting from, or otherwise doing business with Gilder Storage provides no Pick ’Em advantage. Internet access and a free VarsityVue account are required.

3. **Eligibility.** Legal residents of Texas who are at least 18 years old at entry. VarsityVue owners, employees, administrators, moderators, household members, and anyone administering or grading the contest are ineligible. [Confirm operator/household exclusions with counsel.] Void where prohibited. Each entrant must use an account and number they control. One valid entry per natural person for the week, regardless of the number of accounts, devices, or phone numbers.

4. **Contest period and entry cutoff.** Each Week 6–11 slate opens at its separately announced [date/time, America/Chicago], after the approved rules have been published at `/pickem/rules`. When VarsityVue opens a configured weekly contest, it freezes the deadline for **new completed entries** at the then-scheduled kickoff of the earliest included game and displays its exact Central date and time. A complete valid entry must be received **before** that deadline; an incomplete draft cannot become an entry afterward. A later schedule revision does not move the frozen deadline earlier or later. Once new entry closes, existing valid entrants may still edit each pick until that game's individual lock and the Game of the Week prediction until that game's lock. When every included game has locked, all picks are locked. If an included kickoff moves ahead of the frozen entry deadline, VarsityVue must first VOID that matchup for this contest before recording the earlier Pick ’Em lock; no pick in it is required or scored. A postponed or later rescheduled kickoff does not extend the new-entry deadline. All displayed times use America/Chicago, including daylight saving time. [Confirm each actual slate and opening time.]

5. **How to enter.** Create or sign into a free VarsityVue account, provide a U.S. mobile phone number, affirm “I confirm that I am 18 or older, a Texas resident, and agree to the Official Rules,” select a winner for every non-VOID included matchup, predict one integer from 0 to 300 for the **combined points scored by both teams** in the designated Game of the Week, and save. This is a recorded self-attestation, not identity, age, or residency verification. The accepted rules version and attestation time are retained with the completed entry. If the GOTW is VOID before entry, the prediction is omitted and the points tiebreaker is skipped. A success confirmation and an initial completed-entry time indicate receipt. A registration alone, saved draft, partial slate, missing prediction or attestation, unsaved change, or entry submitted at or after the cutoff does not qualify. Draft picks and a draft prediction may be saved before the entry cutoff, but have no contest status, prize effect, or completed-entry timestamp. A phone number’s format is normalized and checked for reuse across accounts. VarsityVue does **not** currently verify that the entrant owns that number or that it is a mobile line. Entrants must keep account and contact details accurate.

6. **Edits and locks.** An entrant who completed a valid entry before the frozen new-entry deadline may change an unlocked pick until that game's lock, even after new entry has closed. The Game of the Week total-points prediction may be edited until that game locks. An edit does not change the original completed-entry time or add a new entrant to the prize count. Locked selections cannot be changed. A failed save leaves the prior valid entry in effect. VarsityVue may correct an administrative scoring or schedule error with an audit trail; entrants do not gain an exception to a past lock.

7. **Scoring.** Each correct pick in a verified, non-void game earns one point. An incorrect pick earns zero. A void game earns neither a point nor a loss. The official winner of a verified forfeit controls that matchup even if no ordinary score is posted. A played overtime game uses its official final winner and full final score, including every overtime period. Authorized verified score corrections can change grading, standings, the Game of the Week prediction comparison, and the provisional winner.

8. **Ranking and tiebreakers.** Rank valid entrants by (a) most correct picks, then (b) the smallest absolute difference between the entrant’s Game of the Week combined-points prediction and the verified combined final score, then (c) earliest initial valid completed-entry time. If tied entries have the same recorded completion time, VarsityVue's recorded entry order resolves the remaining tie. A later edit does not change this order. If the designated Game of the Week has no verified played final with both scores, skip the prediction comparison for everyone and use the initial completed-entry order.

9. **Schedule exceptions and Monday result deadline.** An included game must have a verified official result by **11:59 p.m. Central on the Monday immediately following the contest weekend** to count. Otherwise it becomes VOID for that week's Pick ’Em, even if played later. A verified final recorded by the deadline may receive an authorized score correction later, with grading recalculated. A cancelled game, no contest, or official tie without a pickable winner is VOID for pick scoring. A verified forfeit with an explicit official winner counts for pick scoring; a scoreless forfeit supplies no Game of the Week combined-points total. A postponed or rescheduled game remains pending until a timely verified result or the Monday deadline. If the Game of the Week is VOID or otherwise has no qualifying verified played total, skip the prediction comparison for every entrant and use initial-entry order. The designated Game of the Week cannot be replaced after opening. VarsityVue will post material schedule revisions and contest-VOID decisions.

10. **Prize.** There is one winner per weekly contest. The weekly cash prize in U.S. dollars equals $1 multiplied by the number of **valid accepted completed contest entries** for that week, capped at $100. Thus 17 valid entries yield $17, 54 yield $54, and 100 or more yield $100. Across six separate weeks the maximum advertised prizes could total $600. Registered accounts, partial submissions, disqualified entries, administrator/test entries, and duplicate or fraudulent entries do not count. The displayed accepted-entry count and prize are provisional, subject to eligibility and disqualification review under these rules. Gilder Storage's advertising sponsorship contributes up to $500 total toward the six-week promotion; it does not limit any weekly prize or VarsityVue's obligation to pay advertised prizes. There is no automatic rollover of unused weekly prize capacity. VarsityVue pays each winner; no entry fee is deducted. Payment will use a mutually agreed electronic method, with VarsityVue retaining a reasonable alternative payment option. [Counsel should review payment eligibility and any reporting obligations.] No substitution or transfer except where law requires.

11. **Winner review and notification.** After all included games have verified final or VOID outcomes, VarsityVue checks grading, duplicate and eligibility issues, the Game of the Week total or fallback, and entry order before recording when that week's results were finalized. VarsityVue will initiate contact with the provisional winner using the account email or supplied number within 24 hours after finalization and promptly record when notification was sent. That candidate has 72 hours **from VarsityVue's notification** to respond and establish eligibility as reasonably required under these rules. If the candidate is ineligible, disqualified, cannot be contacted, or fails to respond in time, VarsityVue documents the reason and advances to the next ranked eligible entrant under the **same final ranking**, without a redraw. Each replacement candidate receives 72 hours from their own notification to respond. Before payment, an authorized official score correction that changes the leading eligible entrant requires VarsityVue to review the corrected standings and determine a new provisional winner. A previously contacted or confirmed candidate is not entitled to the prize solely because of that earlier contact or confirmation. VarsityVue retains the earlier notice and review record, records a new results-finalization time after reviewing the corrected ranking, and starts the new provisional winner's ordinary notification and response period. A correction that leaves the leading eligible entrant unchanged does not restart that person's response period. After payment, VarsityVue reviews any correction and resulting standings under these rules and applicable law; it does not automatically demand repayment. [Counsel review of correction and payment remedies.] Reallocation ends 30 days after that week's applicable results finalization. If no eligible winner has successfully claimed the prize by then, the prize is unawarded. No payment is required to claim a prize. Technical failures do not extend a game lock or guarantee receipt of an unsaved submission. If a material outage makes fair administration impossible, VarsityVue will publish an equitable remedy consistent with applicable law rather than silently changing results.
