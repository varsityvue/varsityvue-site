# VarsityVue Week 6 Pick ’Em — proposed Official Rules

**DRAFT FOR OWNER AND LEGAL REVIEW. NOT PUBLISHED.**

The bracketed items require a decision before this can be used. This draft describes the intended mechanics; the Week 6 contest is not open yet.

1. **Sponsor and contest.** The sponsor and operator is [full legal entity name, postal address, and contact email] (“VarsityVue”). The Week 6 Pick ’Em contest is a free football prediction contest. Facebook and Meta do not sponsor, endorse, administer, or associate themselves with it. Entrants release Meta from claims arising from this promotion to the extent permitted by law.

2. **No purchase.** No purchase, payment, donation, subscription, or paid account is required. Buying anything will not improve a participant’s standing. Internet access and a free VarsityVue account are required.

3. **Eligibility.** Legal residents of Texas who are at least 18 years old at entry. VarsityVue owners, employees, administrators, moderators, household members, and anyone administering or grading the contest are ineligible. [Confirm operator/household exclusions with counsel.] Void where prohibited. Each entrant must use an account and number they control. One valid entry per natural person for the week, regardless of the number of accounts, devices, or phone numbers.

4. **Contest period and entry cutoff.** The Week 6 slate opens at [date/time, America/Chicago]. When VarsityVue opens the configured contest, it freezes the entry deadline at the then-scheduled kickoff of the earliest included game. A complete valid entry must be received **before** that stored instant; the exact deadline instant is too late. A later schedule revision does not move that frozen entry deadline earlier or later. Existing entrants may edit only games that remain unlocked, and only before the weekly close [confirm Friday 7:00 p.m. America/Chicago]. The GOTW prediction locks at its game's kickoff. If an included kickoff is moved ahead of the frozen entry deadline, VarsityVue must first VOID that matchup for this contest before recording the earlier Pick ’Em lock; no pick in it is required or scored. The frozen deadline still governs new entries. A postponed or later rescheduled kickoff does not extend it. All displayed times use America/Chicago, including daylight saving time. [Confirm actual Week 6 slate, opening and weekly close.]

5. **How to enter.** Create or sign into a free VarsityVue account, provide a U.S. mobile phone number, select a winner for every non-VOID included matchup, predict one integer from 0 to 300 for the **combined points scored by both teams** in the designated Game of the Week, and save. If that game is VOID before entry, the prediction is omitted and the points tiebreaker is skipped. A success confirmation and an initial completed-entry time indicate receipt. A registration alone, saved draft, partial slate, missing prediction, unsaved change, or entry submitted at or after the cutoff does not qualify. Draft picks may be saved before the entry cutoff, but have no contest status, prize effect, or completed-entry timestamp. A phone number’s format is normalized and checked for reuse across accounts. VarsityVue does **not** currently verify that the entrant owns that number or that it is a mobile line. Entrants must keep account and contact details accurate.

6. **Edits and locks.** A qualified entrant may change an unlocked pick and, before the Game of the Week locks, its total-points prediction. An edit does not change the original completed-entry time. Locked selections cannot be changed. A failed save leaves the prior valid entry in effect. VarsityVue may correct an administrative scoring or schedule error with an audit trail; entrants do not gain an exception to a past lock.

7. **Scoring.** Each correct pick in a verified, non-void game earns one point. An incorrect pick earns zero. A void game earns neither a point nor a loss. The official winner of a verified forfeit controls that matchup even if no ordinary score is posted. A played overtime game uses its official final winner and full final score, including every overtime period. Authoritative verified score corrections can change grading and standings.

8. **Ranking and tiebreakers.** Rank valid entrants by (a) most correct picks, then (b) the smallest absolute difference between the entrant’s Game of the Week combined-points prediction and the authoritative combined final score, then (c) earliest initial valid completed-entry timestamp. If recorded timestamps are identical, the database’s immutable receipt order resolves the remaining tie. A later edit does not change this order. If the designated Game of the Week has no verified played final with both scores, skip (b) for every entrant and use (c).

9. **Schedule exceptions and Monday result deadline.** An included matchup must have an official verified result by **11:59 p.m. America/Chicago on the Monday immediately following the contest weekend** to count. The database uses Tuesday 12:00 a.m. Central as the exclusive cutoff, so all of Monday's 11:59 p.m. minute is included. A game without a qualifying verified terminal outcome by then is VOID for this week's Pick ’Em, even if played later; its canonical game record and eventual score are not invented or erased. A verified final first recorded before the cutoff may receive an authorized score correction later, with grading recalculated. A cancelled game, no contest, or official tie without a pickable winner is VOID for pick scoring. A verified forfeit with an explicit official winner counts for pick scoring; a scoreless forfeit supplies no GOTW combined-points total. A postponed or rescheduled game remains pending until a timely verified result or the Monday deadline. If the GOTW is contest-VOID or otherwise has no qualifying verified played total, skip the points prediction comparison for every entrant and use the initial-entry timestamp. The designated GOTW cannot be replaced after opening. VarsityVue will post material schedule revisions and contest-VOID decisions.

10. **Prize.** There is one winner. The cash prize in U.S. dollars equals $1 multiplied by the number of **valid completed contest entries** for Week 6, capped at $100. Thus 23 valid entries yield $23, while 100 or more yield $100. Registered accounts, partial submissions, disqualified entries, administrator/test entries, and duplicate or fraudulent entries do not count. The displayed amount is provisional until eligibility review and finalization. No entry fee is deducted. Payment will use a mutually agreed electronic method, with VarsityVue retaining a reasonable alternative payment option. [Counsel should review payment eligibility and any reporting obligations.] No substitution or transfer except where law requires.

11. **Winner review and notification.** After all included matchups have verified final or void outcomes, VarsityVue checks grading, duplicate/eligibility issues, the Game of the Week total or fallback, and the original completion order before recording and announcing the winner. VarsityVue will contact the prospective winner using the account email or supplied number within [owner-approved notification interval] after finalization. The prospective winner must respond and supply information reasonably necessary for payment within 72 hours after the first notification. If ineligible, unreachable, or unresponsive, VarsityVue may disqualify the entry and repeat the same ranking procedure. No payment is required to claim a prize. If the prospective winner does not qualify or respond in time, VarsityVue repeats the ranking and contact process with the next eligible entrant. [Confirm notification interval and how long the reallocation process remains open.]

12. **Integrity and technical issues.** VarsityVue may investigate and disqualify duplicate entries, false contact information, account sharing, automated or manipulated submissions, attempts to evade locks, and interference with the contest. A mere formatting difference in the same phone number does not create another entry. Disqualification requires documented administrator review; VarsityVue’s good-faith resolution of scoring and eligibility questions is final to the extent permitted by law. Technical failures do not extend a game lock or guarantee receipt of an unsaved submission. If a material outage makes fair administration impossible, VarsityVue will publish an equitable remedy consistent with applicable law rather than silently changing results.
