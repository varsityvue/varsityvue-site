# Cisco and De Leon take different roads into the playoffs

Draft for owner review, November 8, 2026.

Cisco and De Leon finished district play with the same 4–1 record. Cisco’s 37–20 win at Chesley Field on Friday settled the playoff order, sending the Loboes into the 2A Division I bracket as District 5’s top seed and De Leon in as the second seed.

Cisco opens Thursday against Nocona at Newton Field in Graham. The Loboes are 7–3 and the Indians are 6–4. De Leon opens Friday against Alvord at Tiger Stadium in Jacksboro. The Bearcats are 8–2 and the Bulldogs are 7–3. Both games are scheduled for 7 p.m. Central at neutral sites.

The bracket gives fans of both teams another game to watch Thursday. Cisco’s half of Region II pairs its winner with Bosqueville or Johnson City in the area round. De Leon’s half pairs its winner with Axtell or Bangs. Those second-round pairings depend on the first-round results.

Elsewhere, Goldthwaite brings a 10–0 record to Belton for Thursday’s 7:30 p.m. game against Hubbard. Albany and Crawford both enter Thursday’s 7 p.m. game in Weatherford at 7–3. Stephenville, also 10–0, opens Friday at 7 p.m. against Navasota at Temple’s Wildcat Stadium.
