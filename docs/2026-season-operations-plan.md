## Operating rules

Record status, responsible owner, evidence/source and actual timestamp for each task. Use NOT ASSIGNED / NOT VERIFIED / NOT STARTED rather than guessed completion. Blaine owns the register by default; this does not assign him or Kinsey to every live game. Prior Week 5 coverage/backup assignments do not automatically carry forward. Confirm each week's coverage and backup before Friday.

EDITORIAL FEATURE / GAME OF THE WEEK selects coverage/homepage context; it may be a district preview rather than a single game. PICK ’EM TIEBREAKER GAME is the separately stored contest designation used for combined-point prediction. They can match but neither automatically sets the other. Never modify one just to satisfy the other. Existing save-action coupling needs the separately scoped audit in [Pick ’Em plan](pickem-growth-retention-plan.md).

Freeze new-entry deadline at earliest included kickoff when opening; display precise America/Chicago time. Later reschedules do not extend it; earlier-moved games require the existing VOID workflow. Verify individual game/tiebreaker locks independently. Do not use a generic Friday 7 p.m. assumption for a future slate. Monday unresolved-result cutoff remains Monday 11:59 p.m. Central under current rules; record actual boundary and verified-result evidence. Grade/finalize only through existing verified workflow; do not silently change rules or outcomes.

Winner finalization, actual notice, response and actual payment are separate steps. Notice target: 24 hours after finalization; response: 72 hours from actual notice; reallocation: existing 30-day finalization window. “Record notice already sent” is not sending a notice; “Record prize actually paid” is not payment. Keep private contact/payment evidence in existing restricted systems, not this repository. Before-payment winner-changing correction requires review/re-finalization; post-payment correction has no automatic clawback.

Entry snapshots must separate drafts, complete selections and valid accepted entries with denominators/windows, cutoff time and later eligibility adjustments. Repeat valid-entry snapshot uses stable internal account identity privately and aggregate outputs here. Tagged links identify week, source/campaign and destination; no private identifiers in public URLs. Routine inbox/member support and incidents go here; diagnostic product fixes belong in the roadmap.
