# School Legacy / Trophy Room strategy

Status: POST-SEASON / OFFSEASON — sourced pilot, moved out of NEXT by owner decision September 30, 2026. Priority authority: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

The existing Library `VarsityVue_Archives_Strategy.pdf` (May 11, 2026) is preserved as historical strategic rationale. Its prelaunch/Year 1/Year 2 sequencing is superseded by this horizon; it is not authority for immediate product work. Preserve its lightweight living-archive philosophy and community verification principle without promising comprehensive history.

Start with a small sourced set of school histories/titles/playoff runs. Each claim records year, competition/classification/governing body, source and verification state. Distinguish documented facts, community recollections, disputed claims and unknown gaps. Never infer titles from current standings or present missing archive sections as complete history.

School hubs should expose school Legacy through a clear mobile entry/tab. Retain `/legacy` as directory/discovery, preserve existing stories and URLs or explicit redirects, and link between school histories and directory. Empty sections use honest “History being collected” wording with a reviewed correction/material path.

Archive corrections retain provenance, reviewer and revision history. Historical content cannot rewrite live scores, standings, statistics or contest outcomes through the archive workflow. Current-season preservation and 2027 rollover need separate architecture review; school titles must not be guessed from incomplete current data.

Acceptance: small sourced pilot, verification states, honest gaps, usable school-hub access/directory continuity and no lost material/live-outcome changes. Research capacity and reviewable sources are the major dependencies. Later moderated historical contributions require provenance/display rights, permission, review and audit history; no public wiki editing. Curated greatest-team/player stories require explicit editorial criteria and evidence. Deep cross-school search/database expansion remains DEFERRED until sourced coverage warrants it.
